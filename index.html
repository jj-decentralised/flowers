My Love,

There are mornings when the light comes through the window just right, and I think of you before I think of anything else. Not with urgency, but with the quiet certainty that you are the best part of every day I have been given.

I don't always say it well enough. The words I have feel small next to what I mean. But I want you to know: the way you laugh when you're caught off guard, the way you hold my hand a little tighter when we're walking somewhere new, the way you make even the ordinary feel like something worth remembering — I notice. I always notice.

You make me want to be softer. Braver. More honest with the things I carry. You make the world feel less like something to survive and more like something to be grateful for.

I don't need a special day to tell you this, but I'll take one anyway: thank you for choosing me. Thank you for staying. Thank you for being exactly who you are.

I love you. Completely, quietly, and without condition.

Forever yours, J
